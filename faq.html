Shipping & Insured Delivery

Yes, absolutely. Every Aurelia shipment is 100% fully insured from our vault to your doorstep. We utilize express couriers (FedEx/DHL) and require an adult signature upon delivery. No parcel is left unattended.

We ship to over 60 countries worldwide. Aurelia coordinates all custom brokerage dossiers and handles export duties. Client advisors will reach out directly if regional import permits are requested by customs boards.

Diamond & Gemstone Grading

Every central diamond exceeding 0.50 carats in our collections is certified by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA). You will receive the physical grading report and invoice validation containing laser inscription details.

Our bespoke division sources precious colored gemstones from specific geological origins (e.g., Colombian emeralds, Ceylon sapphires). Specify your origin request on our Private Consultation page.

Returns & Sizing

We provide a 30-day complimentary return or exchange policy on all standard catalog models, provided security tags remain intact. Please note that custom, personalized engraving and bespoke commissions are non-refundable.
