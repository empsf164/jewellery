The Aurelia Spirit

Crafting Brilliance, Honoring Heritage

Founded in the historic jewelry quarters of Antwerp in 1894, Aurelia has stood for a commitment to exquisite craftsmanship, ethical sourcing, and classical design. We believe that fine jewelry is not an accessory, but an heirloom-to-be, housing memories and milestones.

Our artisans train for over a decade in metallurgy and diamond micro-setting to ensure that every crown, claw, and band provides unparalleled comfort and longevity.

Chronicles of Aurelia

The Journey Since 1894

1894

The Antwerp Atelier

Master cutter Jean-Laurent Aurelia opens his boutique storefront in Antwerp, serving royal patrons with cushion-cut diamond commissions.

1935

London Expansion

Aurelia establishes a flagship boutique on Bond Street, introducing bespoke emerald drop collections to London high society.

1998

The Conflict-Free Pact

Aurelia becomes one of the founding members of the international sourcing guidelines, pledging 100% GIA conflict-free diamond tracking.
